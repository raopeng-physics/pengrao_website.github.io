research:
  - title: "Kitaev quantum spin liquids and proximate phases"
    text: 'Quantum spin liquids are theoretical magnetic phases in which the local magnetic moment vanishes even at $T=0$ due to quantum fluctuations. I compute the experimentally relevant signatures of Kitaev quantum spin liquids, e.g. inelastic neutron scattering, using field theory methods.'
  - title: "Interaction-driven phenomena in altermagnets"
    text: "Altermagnets are antiferromagnetically ordered systems with unusual spectral properties, e.g. large spin-split electron bands and split chiral magnon bands. I am mainly interested in interaction phenomena in altermagnets due to the novel quasiparticle spectra."
  - title: "Band topology and anomalous Hall effect"
    text: "In electronic systems with broken time-reversal-symmetry, the bands can acquire integer topological quantum numbers which can lead to a non-zero anomalous Hall conductivity. I study electronic systems with such topological properties."
